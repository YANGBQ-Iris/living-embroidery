The author describes recording liquid in a half-full thermos flask using a Shure SM7B, Triton FetHead and Steinberg UR22C into Audacity. This is a recorded liquid sound, not a traditional Chinese instrument recording.

Local adaptation: mild filtering and quiet short reflections for a calm garden atmosphere; mono PCM WAV at 48 kHz. No pitch sweep, oscillator or synthetic water tone added. Runtime asset: water-drop-natural.wav. Attribution retained voluntarily. Retrieved 25 October 2026.
